How the Reset Takes the Fight Out of Dinner

Instead of a wall of generic tips, you get a simple daily loop: one tiny task, the right words, and a quick log. Here's what makes it work.

Each day gives you a single, low-pressure food exposure tied to a specific meal you're already making. No separate cooking, no overwhelm, just one gentle step.

When your child refuses, you have exact words ready, including what to say and what to avoid, so you stay calm instead of slipping into begging or bribing.

The core line, 'You don't have to eat it,' removes the power struggle. Your child can look, touch, or smell a food with zero pressure to swallow it.

You log each reaction in a quick fill-in box. Watching the ticks add up keeps you consistent on the nights you'd normally want to give up.

By day 14 you'll know exactly which foods and approaches worked for your own child, and you'll have a repeatable loop to keep expanding the list long after.
